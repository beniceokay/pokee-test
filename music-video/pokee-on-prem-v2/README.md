# POKEE — ON-PREM v2 (rotoscoped / 2.5D)

The v2 cut of the music video. Every image is rotoscoped and rebuilt as layers:
- a clean background plate with depth parallax and depth-of-field
- big typography sitting behind the model
- the animated cut-out subject: float, sway, rise, slide-in, zoom-pop on kicks, echo trails, strobe and glitch, with rim light and a soft shadow

## Files
- `footage2.js`: the layer compositor, generated by Pokee Isaac and debugged by Claude. Claude fixed the cover-fit, the zoom direction, the depth lookup, the blur radius and the Y orientation, and rewrote `prepare()` to remove a race.
- `index.html`: the v1 engine (transitions, HUD, lyrics, events, grade). `scripts/integrate.py` patches it to render each shot through `Footage2` into a framebuffer and to split the text into behind/front layers.
- `timeline.js`: the v1 timeline plus per-image assets and subject stats, and per-shot `fg`/`bg` modes and parallax direction.
- `scripts/`: the asset pipeline:
  - `masks.py`: BiRefNet-lite mattes
  - `depth.py`: Depth Anything V2
  - `assets.py`: cut-outs, inpainted plates and subject stats
  - `timeline_v2.py`: writes `timeline.js`
  - `prender2.js`, `renderall2.sh`: rendering
  - `sweep2.js`: full-song error sweep

Fonts come from `../pokee-on-prem/fonts`. The images, cut-outs, plates, depth maps and song are not committed (too large).
