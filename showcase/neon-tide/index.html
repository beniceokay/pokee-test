How to Play

Ride the tide. Keep your resonance alive until the moon answers.
Move

← / → or A / D.
On touch: tap the left or right third of the screen.

Hit

SPACE (or tap the center) as an orb crosses your hit line.
Perfect ±50ms · Great ±100ms · Good ±150ms.

Hold

In the bridge, hold SPACE while the glowing ribbon passes through you.

Dodge

Dark static shards cost −15 resonance. Be in another lane when they arrive.

Tide Mode

Fill the TIDE meter with Perfects, then press SHIFT (or double-tap) to unleash 4 bars of double points and auto-attracting orbs.

Survive

Resonance 0 pulls you under. Esc / P pauses.
